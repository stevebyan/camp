Camp keeps an index of each term you define and each page you publish. You link to them by name,
and Camp finds the URL.

In •page-ref["welcome"]{the welcome post} I defined •term{collections} and •term{source documents}.
Those links go to the definitions. The plural forms resolve to the same terms.

## Define a term

Mark a term as defined at the place you explain it. A •defterm{render function} is the Racket
function that turns a •term{source document} into a page. This site has two, in `render.rkt`.

## When a name does not resolve

If Camp cannot find a term or a page, it marks the reference in the page, and `raco camp build`
logs a warning.

Open `blog/first-post.md.rkt` to see the markup behind these links.
