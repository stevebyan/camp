Welcome to your new site. Camp built it from the plain text files in this folder.

Each file in `blog/` and `pages/` is a •defterm{source document}: a few lines of metadata, then
Markdown. A folder of source documents is a •defterm{collection}, and `site.rkt` says where the
pages of each collection go.

To change how the pages look, edit `render.rkt` and `static/style.css`.
